**Last Updated:** April 2026 | **Status:** Active Research Program

## 🎯 Executive Summary

The market wants CarabinerOS to exist but doesn't believe it will survive. Prediction market simulations show **33.9% confidence** in reaching 250 paying locations within 6 months of Q3 2026 launch. The challenge isn't product-market fit — it's **implementation survival** and **integration depth**.

**Key Insight:** *"The product has a demand problem, not a product problem."* — MiroShark Sim 2 Analysis

## 📊 MiroShark Simulation Results

### Simulation Overview
- **Total Simulations:** 2 (sim_8ea724da, sim_7504cc9e)
- **Polymarket Question:** "Will CarabinerOS reach 250 paying restaurant locations within 6 months of its Q3 2026 public launch?"

### Market Sentiment Analysis

- **Toast** ($200) — Late entry, heavy NO bets

- **Chef Danny** — Final trade at round 96

**🟢 Who Bet YES:**
- **CarabinerOS** ($225) — Buying own market
- **Esteban Nunez** ($50, then sold) — Founder lost confidence late
- **National Restaurant Association** ($140) — Only institutional YES
- **xtraCHEF** ($50, immediately sold) — Brief support

### Critical Market Signals

#### 1. **Integration Depth = #1 Concern**
*"Does it sync bidirectionally with Toast, Square, and existing POS systems?"* — Every media and competitor agent asked this question.

#### 2. **Implementation Survival Risk** 
*"Can an operator adopt this without a dedicated CSM and still be using it at month 6?"* — Product Hunt demanded this metric and never got an answer.

#### 3. **Liability Framework Gap**
*"When the AI hallucinates a PO quantity, who pays?"* — Asked repeatedly, never answered satisfactorily.

## 🏗️ Integration Architecture Intelligence

### Platform Priorities (Based on API Research)

**Tier 1: Build First**
- **Square** — No gates, no approval, broadest surface area
- **Toast** — Market leader, essential integration
- **7shifts** — Labor management critical for ROI

**Tier 2: Partner Track**  
- **Clover** — Requires partner program but accessible
- **OpenTable** — Reservation/covers data for intelligence
- **Lightspeed** — Enterprise segment

**Tier 3: Long-term**
- **DoorDash/Uber Eats** — Delivery optimization
- **Sophisticated logistics platforms** — Advanced operators

### Technical Requirements Summary
- **OAuth flows:** All platforms support standard OAuth 2.0
- **Rate limits:** 100-1000 requests/minute (adequate for restaurant scale)
- **Sandbox access:** Available for all Tier 1 platforms
- **Development cost:** $0-$50/month during build phase

## 📋 Go-to-Market Prescription

### Phase 1: Foundation (Pre-Launch)
1. **Publish liability framework** — AI drafts → human approves → deterministic commit
2. **Total cost transparency** — Include implementation hours, parallel operation period  
3. **Integration depth proof** — Bidirectional Toast/Square sync demo
4. **Kill "Screen 16" narrative** — Video showing tab closure, not addition

### Phase 2: Channel Strategy
1. **Court distributors first** — US Foods showed willingness, reps bypass trust barrier
2. **NRA Show live demo** — Kitchen environment, real operator testimonials
3. **Product Hunt with social proof** — After operator validation, not before

### Phase 3: Metrics Focus
- **6-month retention rate** — Post-CSM withdrawal
- **Implementation survival** — Time to self-sustained usage  
- **Integration uptime** — Bidirectional sync reliability
