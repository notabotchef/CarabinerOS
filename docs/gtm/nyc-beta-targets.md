### Research-backed outreach with personalized demo angles

> **Target archetype (sim-validated):** 3-location independent operator, $2–5M annual revenue per location, not on a national chain POS, currently using some combination of spreadsheets + Toast/Square + MarginEdge or nothing. Will Guidara persona was the most-quoted (39 times) — this is the archetype we're selling to.

> **Process for personalized demos:** Before any outreach, research the target's website, recent reviews (Yelp/Google), menu, Instagram, and any press. Generate a demo database using their real menu items, real suppliers (Chef's Warehouse / US Foods serve most NYC independents), and realistic par levels based on their cuisine type and cover count. Show up with their data, not fake data.

## Target Profile Criteria

- 2–5 locations (enough pain, not enough IT staff)
- Independent (not a franchise or chain with centralized purchasing)
- $3M+ revenue per location (enough volume that 20 min/day in ordering time = real money)
- Currently on Toast, Square, or Lightspeed (integration story is relevant)
- NYC neighborhoods: West Village, Flatiron, LES, Williamsburg, Carroll Gardens (operator-dense, chef-owned)

## Outreach Approach

**Lead with the problem, not the product.**

Opening message formula:
> "We noticed [specific thing about their operation — e.g., 'you're running [X locations] with a rotating seasonal menu']. We built something that reduces the 2 AM ordering grind to 20 minutes. Not a pitch — happy to show you what it looks like with your actual menu and suppliers."

**Never say:** "AI-powered," "revolutionize," "built by a chef" (per sim findings — backfires)
**Always say:** "You approve everything before it goes," "20 minutes," "your data"

## Target Archetypes for NYC

### Archetype 1: 3-Location Neighborhood Italian
**Profile:** 3 locations, seasonal pasta-forward menu, rotating specials board, Chef's Warehouse primary supplier. GM handles ordering across all three locations manually.

**Pain point:** Cross-location inventory sync. When location 2 runs low on '00 flour, location 1 doesn't know.

**Demo angle:** Build a demo with their actual menu items (pull from their website), show the AI drafting a consolidated PO across 3 locations from a single morning check-in.

**Personalization hook:** Reference their seasonal menu on Instagram — show the demo DB has their current specials, not placeholder "chicken breast / romaine."

**Outreach channel:** DM the chef directly on Instagram. Not the restaurant's account — the chef's personal account if public.

---

### Archetype 2: Single High-Volume Wine Bar (expanding to 2nd location)
**Profile:** 1 location currently, announced or rumored 2nd location, $4M+ revenue, complex beverage program, US Foods relationship.

**Pain point:** They're about to double their operational complexity with no additional back-office headcount. This is the exact moment the pain becomes acute.

**Demo angle:** Show what onboarding location 2 looks like — 15 minutes to clone the purchasing setup from location 1, with beverage par levels already populated.

**Personalization hook:** Congratulate them on the expansion before pitching. Be a fan first.

**Outreach channel:** Email the GM directly (usually findable via OpenTable reservation system contact or LinkedIn). Not the info@ address.

---

### Archetype 3: Fast-Casual Multi-Unit (non-chain)
**Profile:** 4–6 locations of a single concept, not franchised, founder still operationally involved, high staff turnover (means ordering is done inconsistently).

**Pain point:** Inconsistent ordering across locations — each location manager orders differently, creating wildly different food costs across units.

**Demo angle:** Show the variance report: what happens when you standardize par levels across all locations vs. what they're experiencing now.

**Personalization hook:** Reference their Google reviews — if food cost inconsistency is causing quality variance, it shows up in reviews ("the Williamsburg location is better than the Flatiron one").

**Outreach channel:** Reach the founder, not the GMs. GMs won't champion a new system. Founders will.

## "Screen 16" Pre-emptive Counter for NYC Demos

MarginEdge's attack line (sim round 53): *"This is Screen 16 — you're adding a 16th dashboard."*

**The counter demo:** Start every NYC demo by asking the operator to show you their current screens. Count them live. Then show CarabinerOS replacing — not supplementing — those screens. The demo must end with the operator closing tabs, not opening a new one.

**The line:** "We close tabs, we don't open them."

## Demo Database Build Instructions

For each target, build a demo DB with:
1. **Their actual menu items** (scraped from their website/menu PDF)
2. **Realistic par levels** based on cuisine type and estimated cover count
3. **Their actual primary supplier** (most NYC independents are Chef's Warehouse or US Foods — check their social media for any chef demos or tagged content)
4. **3 weeks of mock ordering history** so the AI has something to pattern-match against

Time to build per target: ~45 minutes. Worth it. The sim showed the trust curve is set in the first 72 hours — showing up with their data signals you've already done the work.
